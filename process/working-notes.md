# Working notes — where they live and how they are marked

## Where

- **`submissions/`** — captures of discussion and discussion findings not yet placed: an idea from the author, a review synthesis, a capture before research. One file per piece of work.
- **`plan/<topic>/`** — plan documents and research notes for a topic being built. One folder per topic, named in `scheduled-tasks.md`.
- Research notes and captures stand alone (`process/working-rules.md` § Working documents). The only linking layer is `scheduled-tasks.md`.
- When the work a note served is done, the note is removed (on Jules's word); git keeps it.

## Marks

Two kinds of note, two sets of marks. A note states its marks at the top, and the legend in the note is what counts.

- **Captures:** **[A]** the author's position, verbatim or near-verbatim; **[S]** an observation or suggestion from Claude, to be confirmed; **[U]** unverified, to go through a research round before it backs anything.
- **Research notes:** **[C]** checked against a source that was actually opened, with the source named (or "as quoted in" a named secondary source, when the primary was not opened); **[U]** unverified: memory, a search summary, a paraphrase that could not be opened. Never upgrade [U] to [C] without opening the source.

Captures written before 2026-11-24 use **[C]** for Claude's suggestions; their own legend says so, and they are read by it rather than rewritten.
