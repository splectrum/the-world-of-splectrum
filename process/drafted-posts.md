# Drafted posts

Once a blog post is drafted, what happens to it is Jules's call: whether it is worked on, when it is scheduled, and when it goes out.

## How to apply

- Remove a post from `scheduled-tasks.md` as soon as it is drafted, including passing mentions inside another entry. A post not yet written can stay as a plan item until then.
- When listing what is left to do, or deciding what comes next, leave drafted posts out. Don't count them as open work or propose them as the next step.
- Don't suggest dates for posts or point out gaps in the blog schedule.
- Posts parked on 2030 dates count as drafted.
- Touch a post, its tweet or its description only when Jules asks.
