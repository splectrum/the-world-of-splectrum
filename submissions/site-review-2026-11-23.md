First full cold review of the site and repo. Eight reviewers: persons A–G, H–O, P–Z; subjects; SPLectrum's own surfaces (twice, Fable and Opus independently); outward-facing pages and published posts; repo rules. Plus mechanical checks. Drafted posts and Engineering out of scope.

**Cross-check.** All eight reviewers wrote the same picture of SPLectrum's position and direction, independently: the coin (common face / own voice), core values and pillars in two realms, converged subjectivity, hosting not overruling; moving toward a modern natural philosophy, keyword doorways, a widened subject, objectivity as widening convergence, and a leaner library. The two readers of the own-voice surfaces put the same two findings first. The site's direction is legible; the inconsistencies sit underneath it.

**Mechanically the site is sound:** no broken internal links, no upward links from person/subject pages, no relative links, indexes complete.

**The overall diagnosis:** the coin, core values and pillars (Sept–Nov) have outrun the May-era seed, vocabulary, bottom-row and person pages; the rules have grown in layers that now contradict each other; and the person/subject layer still carries four generations of register, with the May pages holding most of the page-voice evaluation and unnecessary detail.
