# SPL Positioning

Terms for engaging external thought languages without imposing on them.

<span id="close-affinity">**Close affinity**</span> — The resonance ring at one remove from the seed: SPLectrum's own positions (the core values, the pillars) read across the field.

<span id="outside-voice">**Outside voice**</span> — The voice of the person and subject pages in [positioning](#positioning). SPLectrum stays out, letting the field speak on its own terms. Contrast: SPLectrum's own voice in the rings, the wider landscape and the core areas.

<span id="positioning">**Positioning**</span> — Outside work that puts SPLectrum in context. Engages external thought languages (thinkers, frameworks, traditions) on [equal standing](/vocabulary/a-z/s/spl-seed/#equal-standing), without imposing SPLectrum's framing.

<span id="positioning-piece">**Positioning piece**</span> — The unit of content in the [positioning](#positioning) area. Each piece engages one thinker, framework, tradition, situational piece, or research angle.

<span id="resonance-and-dissonance">**Resonance and dissonance**</span> — The canonical pair for the relation between a [piece](#positioning-piece) and SPLectrum. Resonance: structural agreement. Dissonance: structural disagreement. The rings show resonance only; where a line of thought does not resonate, its own account stays on its person or subject page.

<span id="wider-landscape">**Wider landscape**</span> — SPLectrum-coloured reports on subject matter, most often a meta subject: SPLectrum's reading of what the neutral pages report.
