# Positioning - The Landscape

Positioning contains reference material on people and subjects across all disciplines of importance to this site. Persons and subjects each have their own A–Z.

**[Persons A–Z](/positioning/persons/)** &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; **[Subjects A–Z](/positioning/subjects/)**

Persons and subjects are held on their own terms — entities at equal standing, independent of any angle taken on them. The same A–Z is then read from more than one angle.

**SPLectrum's readings.** Two resonance rings show where SPLectrum's dynamic surfaces across the field, and coloured reports read whole subject matters through the position.

**[Close affinity](/positioning/close-affinity/)**<br>
