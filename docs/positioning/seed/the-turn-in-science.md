# The Turn in Science

In SPLectrum's philosophy, language is the medium through which a subject — whatever the subject — experiences reality, and shared reality arises from the convergence of subjects. This piece reads across physics, from classical mechanics to [relational quantum mechanics](/positioning/subjects/r/relational-quantum-mechanics/) (RQM), where observations of a like shape turn up in physics' own vocabulary: properties manifest only through interaction, shared facts arise through decoherence, and the observer is any physical system. What follows traces how physics got there.

## The trajectory

**[Leibniz](/positioning/persons/l/leibniz/) (1646–1716)** rejects absolute space entirely — space is not a container, it is the ordering of things that coexist. No space without things. A relational view, but without a mathematical theory it remains scientifically inert. Physics keeps Newton's absolute space for the next two centuries.

**[Maxwell](/positioning/persons/m/maxwell/) (1865)** unifies electricity, magnetism, and light into a single framework of fields and waves. The source of the classical triumph — and its undoing. His equations predict a fixed speed of light — and, combined with classical statistical mechanics, the [ultraviolet catastrophe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraviolet_catastrophe).

**[Planck](/positioning/persons/p/planck/)** (1900) fits the radiation spectrum by letting energy come in discrete packets — the move that removes the catastrophe. **[Einstein](/positioning/persons/e/einstein/)** extends it: light itself comes in quanta, and builds relativity — space and time coupled, gravity as geometry. **[De Broglie](/positioning/persons/d/de-broglie/)** turns it around: particles behave as waves.

**[Heisenberg](/positioning/persons/h/heisenberg/) (1927)** — position and momentum cannot both be definite. Not a limit of instruments, a feature of nature. **[Born](/positioning/persons/b/born/)** — the wave function gives probabilities, not certainties. Classical mechanics tells you what is there. Quantum mechanics tells you what you will find if you look.

**The impasse.** [Bohr](/positioning/persons/b/bohr/) offers complementarity. [Bohm](/positioning/persons/b/bohm/) restores determinism through hidden variables at the cost of non-locality. [Everett](/positioning/persons/e/everett/) proposes all outcomes happen in branching worlds. No consensus.

**[Bell](/positioning/persons/b/bell/) (1964)** shows quantum correlations violate any classical, locally deterministic picture. [Aspect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Aspect) (1982) and others confirm it experimentally — loopholes closed by 2015, [Nobel Prize 2022](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2022/summary/). Nature does not behave classically. Separability fails.

## Relational quantum mechanics

[Rovelli](/positioning/persons/r/rovelli/) (1996) takes what the physics says and drops the artificial observer status. The observer is any physical system. Properties are facts established through interaction. In Rovelli's account, dropping observer-independent facts dissolves the paradoxes.

The connection to [loop quantum gravity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/quantum-gravity/) — where spacetime itself is quantised and relational, with no fixed background — is natural. If spacetime is relational, quantum states should be too.

**Growth.** A small community: Laudisa, Smerlak, [Bitbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Bitbol). The [SEP entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qm-relational/) (2002) gave institutional recognition. In 2010, [van Fraassen](/positioning/persons/v/van-fraassen/) published "Rovelli's World" — identifying the central challenge: if facts are relative, what connects different observers' accounts?

**Decoherence as mechanism.** Early RQM stated the principle: facts are relative. Maturation came through [decoherence](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qm-decoherence/) — the physical process producing stable, shared facts from relational ones. Di Biagio and Rovelli (2021) introduced stable facts — facts whose relativity can be ignored. The classical world is the world of stable facts. But decoherence is itself relational.

**[Frauchiger-Renner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigner%27s_friend#Frauchiger%E2%80%93Renner_thought_experiment) (2018)** sharpened everything. Three assumptions cannot all hold: QM applies universally, measurements produce single outcomes, reasoning transfers between agents. RQM keeps the physics universal and outcomes single — but facts transfer only where decoherence anchors them into a shared context.

## The affinity

The scientific arc runs parallel to the [philosophical one](/positioning/seed/the-turn-in-western-philosophy/): the absolute giving way to the relational — a view Leibniz held for space — carried through Einstein, quantum mechanics, to RQM. Each step extends the turn — Einstein couples space and time, quantum mechanics puts observer-independent properties in question, RQM frames facts themselves as relational. A collective movement, not a point of arrival.
