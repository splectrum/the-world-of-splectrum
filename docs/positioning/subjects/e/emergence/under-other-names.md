# Emergence Under Other Names

The word's definitional condition is documented on [the overviews page](/positioning/subjects/e/emergence/the-overviews/); alongside the literature that runs on the word stands a larger body of work covering the same territory — levels with their own laws, order from interaction, wholes constraining parts, novelty from recombination — in vocabularies of its own, in which the word is not load-bearing even where it appears. This page maps those vocabularies and where each lives on this site.

## Physics: renormalisation, universality, effective theories

Condensed-matter physics carries level-autonomy in working terms of its own. *Universality*: identical critical behaviour across different micro-physics, with the renormalisation group as the exhibited mechanism — the vocabulary in which macro-independence is a theorem rather than a thesis. *Effective theories*: descriptions valid at a scale, with the finer scale's details systematically absorbed. The word appears in this literature — [Anderson](/positioning/persons/a/anderson/)'s essay is the anthology's emblem, and Laughlin and Pines named their protectorates *emergent* — but the technique does its work without it: the renormalisation calculation, not the word, is what carries the claim. The territory lives at [phase transitions](/positioning/subjects/p/phase-transitions/).

## Complexity science: self-organisation, pattern formation, collective behaviour

The complexity sciences run a working vocabulary in which order arising from local interaction has its own names: *self-organisation* (order without external control), *pattern formation*, *collective behaviour*, *criticality*. The distinction between these terms and "emergence" is itself maintained in the field's methodological literature — self-organisation naming the process, emergence the product-to-base relation — and much of the field's working vocabulary is the former. The territory lives at [complex adaptive systems](/positioning/subjects/c/complex-adaptive-systems/), with [Bak](/positioning/persons/b/bak/)'s self-organised criticality and [Prigogine](/positioning/persons/p/prigogine/)'s dissipative structures as its named strands.

## Biology: autopoiesis, organisation, the organism

Biology's account of wholes that are more than assemblies was built in deliberately new vocabulary: *autopoiesis* — the self-producing organisation that makes a living system a unity — coined by [Maturana](/positioning/persons/m/maturana/) and [Varela](/positioning/persons/v/varela/) as its own conceptual apparatus; the *organisation* and *closure* vocabulary that descends from it; and the older *organism* tradition, for which the whole is prior to the analysis of its parts. The territory lives at [autopoiesis](/positioning/subjects/a/autopoiesis) and [the philosophy of organism](/positioning/subjects/p/philosophy-of-organism/), with [basal cognition](/positioning/subjects/b/basal-cognition/) carrying the empirical programme in which cell collectives hold and navigate targets no single cell contains.

## Construction: assembly history

Assembly theory measures complexity by construction history — the depth of what had to exist first — and locates novelty in recombination and selection: a quantitative vocabulary for the arising of the new that runs on *assembly index* and *selection* rather than on the word. The territory lives at [assembly theory](/positioning/subjects/a/assembly-theory/), with [Walker](/positioning/persons/w/walker/) and [Cronin](/positioning/persons/c/cronin/) its principals.

## The social: sui generis, social construction, spontaneous order

The social sciences carried the territory before the word had its modern currency and largely without it. [Durkheim](/positioning/persons/d/durkheim/)'s *sui generis* — social facts exterior to and constraining every individual — is its own coinage, from 1895. *Social construction* names the process by which institutions become objective realities to their members — the territory of [the social construction of reality](/positioning/subjects/s/social-construction-of-reality/). And [Hayek](/positioning/persons/h/hayek/)'s *spontaneous order* — order from human action but not human design — was built as a named alternative to design vocabulary, and does its work without the emergence register.
