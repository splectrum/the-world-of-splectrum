# Life

The word "life" covers a lot of ground. It names what separates a cat from a stone, the whole of the living world, the span between a birth and a death, what a person has lived through, and a way of living. It is also used of stars, civilisations and machines. Biology, chemistry, physics, astronomy, philosophy, history and everyday speech each use the word, and each means something by it. This subject gathers those uses.

Each home has its own questions. What is life, and can it be defined? How did it begin? How far does the living world reach, on this planet and elsewhere? Can it be made? What is it to live a life, to experience it from the inside, and to die? Each of these has a literature of its own.
