# Close affinity

Thinkers and frameworks that share close structural affinity with SPLectrum — different starting points, different vocabularies, arriving at kindred structural territory. Not lineage: resonance.

- [Aesthetics](/positioning/close-affinity/aesthetics/) — aesthetics as the dynamic that completes language-as-medium-of-expression: abstract thinking as that dynamic run in practice, the arts where it runs undisguised, and the scattered field the position gathers (a landing page plus five branch pieces)
- [Basal cognition](/positioning/close-affinity/basal-cognition/) — SPLectrum's widened definition of cognition meets the field studying cognition below the nervous system: research examples of resonance — meaning worked with and created in the non-neural parts of organisms — and the exploration they open, continuity of the cognition process from the non-neural into the neural
- [The three core values](/positioning/close-affinity/core-values/) — belonging, privacy, creativity as a philosophical triad: the four thinkers who hold all three together, and each value carried in depth across its own traditions
- [Conceptual relativity](/positioning/close-affinity/conceptual-relativity/) — the scheme-as-reference-frame dynamic read across Carnap, Quine, Goodman, Kuhn and Putnam: the count with no absolute answer, four arrivals through four materials, and the same move arriving in physics as relational quantum mechanics
- [Epistemology](/positioning/close-affinity/epistemology/) — SPLectrum's knowing as a two-pole loop: disclosed and private in its happening, stabilised by convergence in its holding, read across the disclosive and dissolving stances, the naturalistic turn, and the social turn's communal, situated knower
- [Ethics](/positioning/close-affinity/ethics/) — SPLectrum's ethics as values read off a language game, mandatory for members at the scale of the common, with the shared base between games as the reference for any reading: the form of life that holds its values, norms instituted in a practice, and game-relativity with a shared base
- [Metaphysics](/positioning/close-affinity/metaphysics/) — SPLectrum's metaphysics as disclosure from the subject's seat, relational and with the mirror set aside, and the aspects of that dynamic surfacing across the field's approaches
- [Ontology](/positioning/close-affinity/ontology/) — SPLectrum's entities as the concepts of the language medium, held at rest, read across the framework-relativity line, relations as what constitute an entity, and value-by-difference as the root of the relational
- [Pluralism](/positioning/close-affinity/pluralism/) — the pluralism family engaged from the interrelational position: the count set in motion, the two borders held, and the family's disputes answered from inside
- [Politics](/positioning/close-affinity/politics/) — SPLectrum's politics as the dynamic of coexistence among diverse actors, read off the configuration at every scale and worked from what is held in common: the one-and-the-many as the field's standing concern, arrangements built from the shared, coexistence as ongoing contest, and the single actor as a political site
