# Epistemology

In SPLectrum's philosophy, knowing has two moves held together. Reality is *disclosed* — it shows itself to a subject already in the midst of it, through the language medium the subject is inside — and the experience of it is *private*: there is no looking through another's eyes, no vantage outside one's own. That is the private pole. And what is held true is *stabilised by convergence*: the private experience is fed back into the shared language tools, individual into community through sharing, and what counts as known is what survives that sharing. There is no absolute objectivity, only converged subjectivity — the shared pole. Knowing is the loop between them: disclosed and private in its happening, common and revisable in its holding, fallible throughout, and never finished. SPLectrum holds this core; the derivation is on the reality side, and this surface points outward from it into the field.

Set against the territory — the perennial postures toward knowing that the [metaphysics map](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphysics/) holds as stances, and the [twentieth-century naturalistic turn](/positioning/subjects/e/epistemology/) that renegotiates the field's border with empirical inquiry — this position sits in no single one. What follows points to where the position resonates across the field — two stances carry its two poles, and the modern turn rhymes from a third direction.

---

## The disclosive pole

The private pole is most at home in the disclosive stance — the phenomenological line that starts exactly where SPLectrum starts: not with a theory built about knowledge from outside, but with reality showing itself to a subject already involved in it. [Husserl](/positioning/persons/h/husserl/) returns to the given before any theory is laid over it; [Heidegger](/positioning/persons/h/heidegger/)'s *Dasein* is always already in a world, understanding before it theorises, with truth as unconcealment rather than the matching of a proposition to a fact; [Merleau-Ponty](/positioning/persons/m/merleau-ponty/) seats the giving in the body; [Gadamer](/positioning/persons/g/gadamer/) in the situated interpreter for whom there is no neutral vantage. The resonance is the disclosure itself, and the privacy that comes with it: experience is given from a seat that no one else occupies, and there is no outside view even in principle. This is the pillar's *how knowing happens* — and here is a whole approach for which it is the way in too.

The same tradition meets the other side of privacy as a problem of its own: intersubjectivity — how a genuinely shared world arises among subjects, none in another's seat. Husserl works at it in the fifth of his *Cartesian Meditations*, Heidegger answers with being-with (*Mitsein*) as part of being-in-the-world, Merleau-Ponty with the intercorporeality of bodies that perceive one another. And the reading carries on to the second pole: reaching a shared world runs through the language medium, by the same convergence through which any concept comes to be held in common.

## The dissolving pole

The shared pole rhymes with the dissolving stance — the pragmatist line that sets the mirror aside and locates what holds true not in correspondence to a ready-made reality but in what survives shared inquiry. [Peirce](/positioning/persons/p/peirce/)'s truth as the convergence inquiry tends toward in the long run, [Dewey](/positioning/persons/d/dewey/)'s knowing as the settled outcome of inquiry, [Rorty](/positioning/persons/r/rorty/)'s refusal of a vocabulary that reports the world as it really is — these are the shape of *converged subjectivity*: no absolute standard above the sharing, the common ground reached and revised rather than read off. The fallibilism is shared too: what is held true is held provisionally, open to the next round.

Within the line, the convergence sits in different places. Peirce lodges it in the community of inquirers and the opinion it is fated to reach; Dewey in the settling of a problematic situation; Rorty in conversation itself — the vocabulary a community happens to share, with nothing above it to answer to. And the reading carries on beneath inquiry: discussion runs on concepts that have already converged, so the convergence of inquiry rides on a convergence already held in common language.

## The naturalistic turn — warrant in the loop

The [naturalistic turn](/positioning/subjects/e/epistemology/) relocates warrant out of the believer's reflective access and into the process that produced the belief — the externalist move, reliabilism at its hinge: what justifies is the reliability of the process, whether or not the knower can certify it from inside. The dynamic surfaces in the relocation itself: what holds true is not what a solitary reasoner can ground from the armchair but what a process yields — warrant in the process, not in a private act of reflective certification. [Quine](/positioning/persons/q/quine/)'s "Epistemology Naturalized" (1969) hands the process to empirical psychology; Alvin Goldman's process reliabilism (1979) makes justification a matter of the belief-forming process's track record. And the same relocation reads on into the loop of private experience fed back into shared tools.

## The social turn — knowing as communal

The social turn's first strand is a clean rhyme. Knowing is irreducibly communal: most of what anyone knows is held on the word of others, through testimony and a division of cognitive labour, certified in common — and there is no view from nowhere. That is converged subjectivity from the side of its machinery, and belonging-first from the side of the knower: the individual always already among others, knowing through them.

The standpoint strand carries the situatedness. Standpoint theory (Sandra Harding), Donna Haraway's "situated knowledges" (1988) and Miranda Fricker's work on epistemic injustice (2007) hold that knowing is conditioned by where the knower stands, with no neutral vantage: the knower always situated, and the situation part of what is known.

---

## How it straddles

Across the territory the dynamic surfaces in pieces. Disclosure carries the private pole — knowing as given to a subject already in the midst of it, from a seat no one else holds; the dissolving stance carries the shared pole — convergence as what stabilises, the mirror set aside; the naturalistic turn carries the relocation of warrant into process; the social turn carries communal, situated knowing. Held together they make one move: knowing as the loop of private, disclosed experience fed into shared language and converging — fallible, never finished, no absolute and no mirror.
