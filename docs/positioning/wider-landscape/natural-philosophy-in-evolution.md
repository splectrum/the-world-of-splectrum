# Natural Philosophy in Evolution

SPLectrum takes knowledge and understanding to be generated by cognition, in a medium shared by everything that takes part in it. On that view the knower is part of nature, and knowing is something nature does. Read from there, natural philosophy has evolved with the ways of knowing, and its evolution shows a steady narrowing: each time the way of knowing changed, what counted as nature changed with it, and what fell outside nature fell to philosophy. Russell's residue, the part of philosophy no science has taken, is what was left when the knower had been put outside.

**The whole.** The first Greek thinkers called their work inquiry into nature, *historia peri physeos*, and nature was the whole: the moon, the sun, the stars and the origin of the universe. Pythagoras, asked what human beings are born for, said "to be an observer of the sky". Knowing was seeing and inquiring, and the one who looked was simply inside what was looked at. ([The inquiry into nature](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphilosophy/greece-and-rome/inquiry-into-nature/))

**A branch.** [Aristotle](/positioning/persons/a/aristotle/) made physics one of three theoretical philosophies, beside mathematics and theology, and gave it things that move. Knowledge rose from perception, through memory and experience, to principles. The knower stood half inside: the student of nature studies the soul only "so much of it as is not independent of matter". ([Philosophy in Greece and Rome](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphilosophy/greece-and-rome/))

**The curriculum.** In the schools of the Islamic world and the Latin West, natural philosophy became a set of books. Its subject was *ens mobile*, movable being, for [Aquinas](/positioning/persons/a/aquinas/), and Aristotle's *Physics* was called *On Natural Hearing*, because it was handed down by teaching. Revelation was set apart in the faculty of theology. Knowing nature now meant reading and hearing an author, with experience, in Roger Bacon's word, at the edge. ([The schools and the universities](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphilosophy/western/schools-and-universities/), [Natural philosophy](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphilosophy/western/natural-philosophy/))

**The seventeenth century.** Three moves came together. [Galileo](/positioning/persons/g/galileo/) read the book of nature in the language of mathematics, and put tastes, odours and colours into the sensing body. [Descartes](/positioning/persons/d/descartes/) set the thinking thing apart from extended matter and made physics geometry: "toute ma Physique n'est autre chose que Géométrie". Bacon and [Newton](/positioning/persons/n/newton/) made experiment and method the way in. God and final causes were still inside natural philosophy for [Locke](/positioning/persons/l/locke/) and Newton, but the knower had left. As Collingwood put it, minds came to "form a class of beings outside nature". ([The new philosophy](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphilosophy/western/new-philosophy/))

**Division and residue.** [Kant](/positioning/persons/k/kant/) made the mathematical the measure: there is "as much science proper … as there is mathematics in it". The workers on nature took a name of their own, "scientist", and natural philosophy came to mean physics. Knowledge split into the sciences of nature and the human sciences, laws and events. [Russell](/positioning/persons/r/russell/) then gave the rule for what was left: as soon as definite knowledge of a subject becomes possible, it "ceases to be called philosophy". ([The nineteenth century](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphilosophy/western/nineteenth-century/), [Philosophy and the special sciences](/positioning/subjects/m/metaphilosophy/western/philosophy-and-the-special-sciences/))

Read through the position, the residue is not arbitrary. It is the complement of the narrowed nature: what the method could not take, with the knower at its centre. [Collingwood](/positioning/persons/c/collingwood/) said it from inside the history: what is "extruded from the concept of nature must find a lodgement somewhere else … first, qualities in general; secondly, minds". Burtt found that the central place of the theory of knowledge in modern philosophy "is no accident", and [Whitehead](/positioning/persons/w/whitehead/) that science's abstractions had been "foisted onto philosophy". Comte reads the same history differently: for him the mind cannot observe itself, and its study goes to physiology. The philosophies of the special sciences that followed stand outside the sciences' nature and look in.

The step back runs the other way. If the knower is part of nature, and knowing happens in a medium that every voice shares, then nature can again be the whole, and what was left over as the residue is at its centre. That is the ground SPLectrum's account of knowing works from ([Epistemology](/reality/epistemology/)).
