# The seed and Historicity

This page builds on the [original principles](/seed/original/) and focuses on P2 — subject experience and reality. Without historicity, differentiation could be static — a difference that leaves no trace, no subject, no experience. Historicity is what makes the seed work.

**P0 — Being implies language.**<br>
**P1 — Language is relational.**

As per the [original principles](/seed/original/).

**P2 — Language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality.**

P2 places the subject inside the relational: no outside view, no perspective from nowhere. A subject's reality is the totality of what it has retained — and so retention is what makes something a subject in the first place.

Without retention, there is interaction but no experience: things happen and nothing is changed by them. With it, traces of interaction are carried forward. This is historicity, the capacity to retain. The trace accumulates: retention becomes memory, memory enables interpretation, interpretation builds reality. It stretches a point of existence into duration. Retention carries the past into the present; anticipation — shaped by what has been retained — projects the present into the future. The present is never an isolated instant — it is a span that carries the past and leans into the future.

The two directions are inseparable: what has been retained shapes what can be reached toward, and what is reached toward reshapes how the retained is understood. Every new understanding starts from what has already been retained and interpreted — not a bias to overcome, but the ground from which anything new can be approached at all. The past does not sit behind the subject; it shapes the horizon through which the future becomes visible.

Without historicity, existence is a point: bare differentiation, constancy. With it, the point becomes a span that reaches in both directions.
