# P5 A Web of Complexity 101

**P5 — Together they form a web of growing complexity.**

Languages don't just sit side by side. They meet, and where they meet, something new can start. Cooking meets chemistry and you get food science. Texting meets the human face and you get emoji. Each new language brings new relations, and new places to meet. So the web grows, and not only bigger: denser.

What makes it grow is us, and every other subject. Reality opens up to us through language, and we don't just look on. We experience, we share, and each time something can shift: a new word, a new habit, a new way of counting. What gets picked up spreads, and becomes part of the reality the next one meets.

That is evolution: no plan unfolding, nobody outside drawing the map, just subjects living and sharing in language, and a web of relations growing ever more complex. Reality is not a finished thing. It is on the move, and we are moving it.
