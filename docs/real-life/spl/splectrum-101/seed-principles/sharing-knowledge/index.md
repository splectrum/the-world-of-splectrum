# P3 Sharing Knowledge About Reality 101

**P3 — Language is where subjects share knowledge about reality.**

Knowledge, in plain words, is information that means something: the ice is thin, the bread is fresh, someone is at the door. It lives in experience, in how we relate, and it is expressed in language.

When we are close to each other, we share a lot without trying: how we look, sound, move. Call it basic passive sharing.

But we kept adding to it, actively: a pointing finger, a song. Each new language lets us share differently on top of what was shared before. As humans, our exchange has evolved more and more in the abstract: a calendar, money, a timetable. All of it is reality now: our reality. As a species, look how we have reshaped that reality.

This is not subjectivism, reality as whatever you happen to feel, but a solid convergence on what has settled out of our sharing.
