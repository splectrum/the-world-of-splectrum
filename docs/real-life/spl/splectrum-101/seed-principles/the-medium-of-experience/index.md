# P2 The Medium of Experience 101

**P2 — Language is the medium through which a subject experiences reality.**

Nothing exists that isn't witnessed by something or someone. To exist means to be in relation to other things: to be witnessed by subjects. And that relation is expressed in language. A subject needn't be a person: it could be a stone in the sun. There is no reality standing on its own, behind the relations, for us to describe or experience. The experience is the reality.

Isn't that subjectivism, reality as whatever you happen to feel? It would be if we were unrelated. The principles that follow take that worry up.

But this principle does tell you something important: your relationship with reality is personal. No one experiences for you. The way you experience reality is you. And that relationship is also yours to shape: not reality at will, but how you meet it. To experience is a continuous process.

[Wittgenstein](/positioning/persons/w/wittgenstein/) ended his *Tractatus* (1921) with the line: "Whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent." Earlier in the same book he wrote: "The limits of my language mean the limits of my world." SPLectrum arrives at its own version: about what relates to nothing, nothing can be said.
