# SPLectrum 101

SPLectrum in one line: **reality disclosed in language.**

Look around, listen, touch something. Whatever reaches you arrives already meaning something: the cup is on the table, the voice is behind you, the milk has turned. You never get a raw world first and work out what it is afterwards. Reality engages with you in your language: it speaks what you understand.

And "language" here is much more than words. It is the whole of how you interact, and it works like a game. Your life is full of such games: how to be a taxi driver, a parent, a friend.

SPLectrum is built on a foundation of six principles, [the seed](/seed/original/). The one line condenses those principles.

- [The Seed Principles 101](/real-life/spl/splectrum-101/seed-principles/) — the six principles one by one, in plain words.

SPLectrum calls itself the home of [interrelational pluralism](/seed/interrelational-pluralism/), and for a good reason. It sees all voices as having equal standing, and cross-pollination between viewpoints as a major driver of knowledge and understanding.

Because of this pluralist stance, it presents itself with two faces, like a [coin](/seed/pluralism-and-the-coin/): on one side it talks about what it finds common to all voices, on the other side it speaks with its [own voice](/reality/).

- [What is held in common](/real-life/spl/splectrum-101/common-face/) — the first face of the coin: what every voice shares.
- [SPLectrum's own voice](/real-life/spl/splectrum-101/own-voice/) — the other face: the seed principles applied further, into a voice of its own.

Before it speaks, SPLectrum listens across a broad spectrum. It sees philosophy as a modern natural philosophy: the comprehensive study of all knowledge. Though a specialised discipline, philosophy should not reside in an ivory tower. Listening first is what the large [reference library](/positioning/) on this site reflects.
