# Evolution homes: analysis for the wider-landscape piece

Research input for the evolution wider-landscape proposal. Every home on the evolution subject is read against the same questions. Each cell is drawn from that home's page on splectrum.world, or marked *page silent*. The two mineral pages (the hub branch and the mineral evolution subject) are read together, and assembly theory is read from its subject landing only. Progress and direction is not a home: it is used in the cross-reading, where it holds the material on direction.

Quotations come from page transcriptions made with a fetch tool. Most were confirmed on a second fetch; the exceptions are noted under *Fidelity notes*.

## Vantage key

Vantage means where the account of change is built from.

- **Own course.** The change is followed through the thing's own course in time, as read from the thing that changes.
- **Compared instances.** One course is assembled from outside by setting many instances side by side at different stages.
- **Lineage.** A population or lineage is tracked across generations.
- **Whole.** The whole (universe, world, society as such, technology as a whole) is taken as one unit.
- **Rule.** A law, rule or designed procedure describes the change from outside.

## Cross-reading

### 1. Vantage is rarely single

Almost every home builds its account from more than one place. Three cases show it most clearly:

- **Stellar evolution** reads compared instances, uses models that follow one star, and tracks generations.
- **Language change** holds all five vantages.
- **Technology** holds two scales, which its page names itself.

Homes with a single dominant vantage are the exception: evolutionary computation (rule) and cosmic evolution, apart from Smolin (whole). The piece cannot assign one vantage per home. It has to record vantage claim by claim.

### 2. The fields name vantage in their own words

The vantage distinction is not imposed from outside. The pages show the fields drawing it themselves:

- **Stars:** "read off many stars observed at once".
- **Civilisations:** "laying the cases side by side at corresponding stages".
- **Assembly theory:** "relocate complexity from object-properties to object-trajectories".
- **Evolutionary computation:** "the experimenter's judgement where the environment stands".
- **Kuhn:** "directed-looking change … with no goal set in advance".
- **Hull:** a "participant-observer's history".
- **Haff:** "the people inside it cannot control".
- **Saussure:** static and evolutionary axes.
- **Veblen:** equilibrium against process.
- **Gould:** mean against mode.
- **Nisbet:** "the direction came with the metaphor, and the metaphor came from the organism".

So the piece can carry vantage in the fields' own language.

### 3. Direction follows vantage, as a pattern rather than a rule

In the material, directions claimed as built in are mostly read from the whole, from a rule, or from compared instances:

- Spencer's law, Comte and White (rule).
- Huxley's "directional and essentially irreversible" (whole).
- Teilhard (whole).
- Kelly (whole).
- The stage ladder (compared instances).
- Mineral evolution's "deterministic" sequence (rule).

Read along a thing's own course or along a lineage, direction is mostly recorded as an outcome or denied:

- Darwin to Hyatt.
- Gould's mode staying with the bacteria.
- Kuhn.
- Bergson's "no plan".
- Toynbee and Diamond.
- Lenski's "blind and purposeless".
- Chemical selection, where the best copiers "take over".

The exceptions are real. A star's own course is "a single irreversible path … fixed mainly by its mass", and Spengler fixes the course within each instance. In both, the direction is fixed per instance by a starting quantity or a type, and claimed for no history beyond the instance.

The material also records the move itself, in two places:

- Spencer "took the law of individual development from von Baer's embryology … and made it the law of everything". An own-course reading became a rule for the whole.
- The biology page: "Embryology also supplied the pattern the species sense later took over".

### 4. Unit disputes are vantage disputes

Many of the field's arguments over the unit are arguments over where change is read from:

- Lewontin's units of selection; Hull's replicators and interactors.
- Boas against the ladder ("each culture … through its own").
- Sahlins and Service on general against specific evolution.
- Bradie's two programmes, which "can succeed or fail independently".
- Technology's two scales.
- Memetics "from the meme's point of view" against populations and against Sperber's minds.
- Saussure's axes.

The piece can let these disputes stand as the fields' own versions of the vantage question.

### 5. Contingency has three locations

- **Starting conditions,** in the law-given and per-instance homes:
  - the initial state, and the early universe's low entropy (physics);
  - a star's mass;
  - the early atmosphere (chemistry);
  - Gould's starting wall.
- **Response, decision, position and circumstance,** in the human homes:
  - Toynbee and Diamond;
  - Quigley's reform or circumvention;
  - Edith Penrose's deliberate choices;
  - firms that "happen to realise positive profits";
  - Godfrey-Smith's success by "position";
  - Hayek's "not a rational process".
- **Chance and variation:**
  - Calvin's "random process, so far";
  - copying errors;
  - Smolin's slight variations;
  - tychasm;
  - the dice of the first evolution strategies;
  - Bergson's "unpredictable in principle".

Only one home locates contingency in design: evolutionary computation.

### 6. Constraints come in three kinds, beside the contingency

- **Fixed by a starting quantity:** mass fixes a star's path; Gould's wall.
- **Caps on carrying:**
  - the error threshold;
  - low-fidelity cultural transmission;
  - Weismann's barrier;
  - epigenetic marks possibly reset each generation.
- **Limits on range:**
  - mineral evolution "not open-ended";
  - three stellar endpoints;
  - reducing conditions before oxygenation;
  - Basalla's "no radical breaks";
  - Haff's dynamics that "the people inside it cannot control";
  - Witt's continuity, "constrained by" biology.

So the material supports "contingent within constraint" home by home, without argument.

### 7. Carrying is mostly accumulation, with copying as one case

Accumulating stock is the most widespread answer in the material:

- Minerals: "species accumulate against the stock built by earlier ones".
- Star generations enriched in heavy elements.
- Tainter's layers of organisation.
- Lenski's technological information.
- Basalla's antecedents and Arthur's components.
- Veblen's "cumulative change".
- "each stage building on the last" (cosmic evolution).

Copies with errors belong to chemistry, biology, Smolin and evolutionary computation. Reconstruction (Sperber), institutions and routines, and the state (physics) are the other answers.

### 8. Traction is thin where change is given by law or accumulation

Physics, stellar evolution and mineral evolution say little or nothing about spread or dominance. Their change is given or accumulates. The richest traction answers come from the human homes and chemistry: biases, attraction, propagation, imitation, market selection, persistence, design. This is an edge to show, not a gap.

### 9. Couplings run both ways

- **Hand-overs:**
  - chemistry to descent;
  - physics to chemistry, through Prigogine and Eigen.
- **Shared histories:**
  - minerals and the biosphere as "a single coupled history";
  - genes and culture interacting.
- **Continuity against analogy:**
  - Witt against generalised Darwinism;
  - assembly theory's unbroken lineage;
  - Haff's geologic stage.
- **Borrowing in both directions:**
  - linguistics had "genealogies of descent before biology did";
  - Peirce read Darwinism as political economy;
  - Marshall's "Mecca … in economic biology".

### 10. Vantage cuts across the hub's two senses

At first sight vantage lines up with the hub's two senses. The unfolding sense is read along an own course, from compared instances or from the whole, and descent is read along a lineage. The crossings are many:

- lineage inside stellar evolution (star generations) and inside cosmic evolution (Smolin);
- the hand-over within chemical evolution;
- Schumpeter's own-course development inside economics;
- Lamarck as the hinge between an own course and a lineage;
- language change holding every vantage.

Vantage is a cut of its own, not a restatement of the hub's senses.

### 11. Silences

- Biology records no constraints.
- The stage schemes are silent on traction.
- Assembly theory's landing says nothing on direction and holds its contingency claim on a sub-page.
- Most homes are silent on what is carried between instances (between civilisations, between stars before galactic chemistry).

## Implications for the proposal

- **Restructure question 2.** Make it "What changes and what does it carry, read from where?" Direction and the carried past are both read from the entity that changes, with the vantage recorded each time.
- **Direction gets no question of its own.** It is reported under question 2, always with its vantage: as an outcome read along a course or lineage, or as a claim read from the whole, a rule or compared instances. Findings 3 and 10 carry this.
- **Use the fields' own vantage vocabulary (finding 2),** so that the vantage distinction stays field-owned and plain.
- **Locate contingency with its constraints in each home (findings 5 and 6).** This replaces the earlier law-and-history cut.
- **Lead question 2's carrying material with accumulation (finding 7),** with copying as one case among others.
- **Unit disputes become content under question 2 (finding 4).**
- **Edges.** Evolutionary computation (rule-built, with contingency located in design), metaphysics (the whole as unit) and the thin traction answers of the law-given and accumulating homes (finding 8).
