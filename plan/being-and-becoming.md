## The seed piece — discussion, NOT settled

- **The formulation.** *Being implies becoming*, as a rephrase of P0 ("being implies language"). "Implies" rather than "is": the same verb as P0, and no "who is first".
- **Working reading.** Being as the concept (static), becoming as the language game that expresses the context (dynamic) — two faces held together, consistent with *Being as tension*, not becoming absorbing being (process philosophy's core move).
- **What makes it the position's own.** Becoming *in language*. This is the observation now wrongly on the Process philosophy subject (below); its destination is this piece.
- **Why it matters.** With language as the medium, evolution is in the evolving languages, which feed back into a changed concept landscape: becoming in the language game reshapes the static side, which the next round of language games expresses. The reality page `/reality/evolution/` reads the curve as growth in expression (P5) but does not state the feedback loop; check the two agree when the piece is written.
- **Constancy does not exclude evolution.** Homo sapiens has evolved and is still homo sapiens; on the working reading the kind is the static face, the population the dynamic.
- **Concepts in motion.** The shared language does not hold a concept steady: a concept can evolve language-game-wise and remain the same concept in name; or the change can transform it into a new state of concepts, a transition between context landscapes with a discontinuity whose realisation has an abrupt feel (the QM-collapse image as indication only; relational-only guard if ever written; phase transitions is the site's neighbour). Where the discontinuity lies is not for the seed to answer — it is found within the dynamic of the languages, case by case. The word "evolution" is itself a case. Field names, to check: Kuhn on "mass", the gestalt switch, incommensurability; Thagard's *Conceptual Revolutions* (1992); Eldredge and Gould's punctuated equilibrium.
- **Open.** What the static face is if content drifts under a constant name: the name, or the concept as it stands at a moment. And the Historicity seed line "without historicity, existence is a point: bare differentiation, constancy" — on the working reading it may stand as the static face, or need rewording.
