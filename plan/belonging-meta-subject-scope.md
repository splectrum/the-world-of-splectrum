# Scoping: the belonging meta subject

Working document, opened 2026-09-13. Provisional throughout. A *scoping* document: it states what the doorway would gather, what has to be built before it can exist, and what is undecided. It does not do the research and it does not settle surfaces.

Built under the keyword-doorway strategy (`process/keyword-doorways.md`) — belonging as the third instance after cognition and emergence, and the first one built *as* a strategy rather than for local reasons.

## 1. Why belonging

It is a SPLectrum keyword with a standing the first two instances did not have: one of the three core values, so the position already holds it central and the close-affinity triad page already exists. The doorway would not be positioning an outside concept but gathering the field around something the position takes as constitutive.

And the field is genuinely scattered: biology and anthropology, social and developmental psychology, sociology, nationalism studies, political theory. Different vocabularies, no shared definition, work that rarely cites across the divides — the condition that made the first two doorways worth building.

## 2. Rule 5 applies, and it is the first thing to settle

Cognition and emergence are words the field itself uses heavily; the doorway gathered work that names itself. **Belonging is not.** The field's own words are *membership*, *group identity*, *social identity*, *solidarity*, *inclusion*, *attachment*, *social integration*, *citizenship*. "Belonging" appears in the psychology of the *need* (Baumeister's belongingness) and in recent applied literature (belonging interventions in education), but it is not the umbrella term for the territory.

Consequences, to be decided before building:
- The scope definition does more work than in the first two instances, because it has to say what collection the word marks when the literature uses other words.
- The bundle may need a page for the territory carried under other names, as the emergence bundle has — or the facets may absorb that job by being named in the field's vocabulary rather than ours.
- **Open question:** is "belonging" the right name for the doorway, or is the territory better named as the field names it (membership? group identity?) with belonging as the position's word linking in? The territory-naming test from the phase-transitions call applies. Naming it *belonging* is defensible precisely because it is the keyword and the doorway exists to serve the keyword — but the call should be made, not drifted into.

## 3. What the doorway would gather — candidate facets

Unverified, unordered, and deliberately more than will survive. Each facet needs backing before it can be pointed at.

**Psychology — the group and the need.** Social identity theory: Tajfel and Turner (minimal groups, self-categorisation); Marilynn Brewer (optimal distinctiveness — the tension between fitting in and standing out, which is structurally interesting for the position); Baumeister and Leary on belongingness as a fundamental need; the developmental line on early intergroup preference (Kinzler on accent, Dunham on minimal groups in children, Bigler & Liben's developmental intergroup theory, Rhodes on essentialism as culturally transmitted). Note: the developmental material was verified during the Moffett round — capacity early, content environmental, preference not hostility.

**Biology and anthropology — the mechanism of membership.** Moffett (built): markers, anonymous vs individual-recognition societies, the affectively neutral boundary. Tomasello and Henrich (both built) on the human-social-evolution side. Non-territorial membership: secret societies, initiation societies (Poro, Sande), diasporas, orders, trade networks — the cluster the union-formation thread needs.

**Sociology — integration and solidarity.** Durkheim (built): social facts, mechanical and organic solidarity, integration. Michael Hechter on group solidarity. The social-construction-of-reality subject (built) is adjacent.

**Nationalism and the imagined community.** Renan (the daily plebiscite), Benedict Anderson (imagined communities, print capitalism — currently external on the Moffett page), Gellner, Hobsbawm on invented tradition, Anthony D. Smith's ethnosymbolist counter, Brubaker.

**Political theory — membership as a question of justice.** Walzer on membership (*Spheres of Justice* ch. 2), the citizenship literature, consociational and non-territorial-jurisdiction arrangements (millet systems, personal law).

**Attachment.** Bowlby and Ainsworth — belonging at the dyadic scale, which may or may not belong in the same doorway. **Open question:** is attachment the same territory or a different one that shares a word?

## 4. Backing state — thin, and this is the governing fact

Checked 2026-09-13. **Exists:** Durkheim, Moffett, Tomasello, Henrich, Taylor, Bakhtin, Whitehead, Nishida (the last four via the core-values close-affinity page), the social-construction-of-reality subject.

**Does not exist:** Tajfel, Turner, Brewer, Baumeister, Walzer, Renan, Gellner, Hobsbawm, Benedict Anderson, A. D. Smith, Brubaker, Hechter, Bowlby — i.e. none of the psychology cluster, none of the nationalism cluster, none of the political-theory cluster.

So by rule 2 (backing before doorway) **the doorway cannot be built yet, and this is a phase rather than a page.** The order:

1. Decide the naming and scope question (§2).
2. Build backing, cluster by cluster, each on its own merits and reviewed as usual. The person-page-earns-on-importance rule applies to every name above — none of them is admitted by appearing on this list.
3. Only then the meta landing, with facets that point at what exists.

Which clusters first is a collaborative call. The psychology cluster (social identity + belongingness) is the largest gap and the most-cited territory; the nationalism cluster is what the union-formation thread needs.

## 5a. The shape to expect: three surfaces (Jules, 2026-09-13)

Belonging will land as the same set emergence did — **meta subject + close affinity + reality side** — and planning it that way from the start is the point of having a strategy. The doorway is neutral and is the way in; the close-affinity piece carries the resonance in the resonance-only register; the reality-side piece carries the position's own formulation. Three jobs, not three versions of one, with links running from the position's surfaces down into the doorway.

So the keyword is not finished when the doorway is built. It is finished when all three stand and agree — which is also the check on whether the backing was ever sufficient.

## 6. The position's own reading stays out

The doorway is neutral. SPLectrum's reading of belonging — the core value, the gate-vs-graded question, what a marker costs to hold — belongs elsewhere: the **owed belonging close-affinity piece** (already deferred to in public by the Moffett person page and two posts), and whatever reality-side material the core value warrants. Those link down into the doorway; the doorway does not link up.

**Open question:** does the owed close-affinity piece come before or after the doorway? It has been promised in public, which argues for sooner; it also rests on backing that does not exist yet, which argues for later. Not to be decided by drift.
