## The core — the position's formulation (discussion, NOT settled)

- **The formulation.** *Being implies becoming*, as a rephrase of P0 ("being implies language"). "Implies" rather than "is": the same verb as P0, and no "who is first".
- **Working reading.** Being as the concept (static), becoming as the language game that expresses the context (dynamic) — two faces held together, consistent with *Being as tension*, not becoming absorbing being (process philosophy's core move).
- **What makes it the position's own.** Becoming *in language*. This is the observation now wrongly on the Process philosophy subject (below); its destination is this piece.
- **Why it matters.** With language as the medium, evolution is in the evolving languages, which feed back into a changed concept landscape: becoming in the language game reshapes the static side, which the next round of language games expresses. The reality page `/reality/evolution/` reads the curve as growth in expression (P5) but does not state the feedback loop; check the two agree when the piece is written.
- **Constancy does not exclude evolution.** Homo sapiens has evolved and is still homo sapiens; on the working reading the kind is the static face, the population the dynamic.
- **Concepts in motion.** The shared language does not hold a concept steady: a concept can evolve language-game-wise and remain the same concept in name; or the change can transform it into a new state of concepts, a transition between context landscapes with a discontinuity whose realisation has an abrupt feel (the QM-collapse image as indication only; relational-only guard if ever written; phase transitions is the site's neighbour). Where the discontinuity lies is not for the seed to answer — it is found within the dynamic of the languages, case by case. The word "evolution" is itself a case. Field names, to check: Kuhn on "mass", the gestalt switch, incommensurability; Thagard's *Conceptual Revolutions* (1992); Eldredge and Gould's punctuated equilibrium.
- **Foundations that rely on non-evolution** *(Jules, 2026-10-06)*. Evolution is incompatible with a lot of thinking: it corrodes its foundations. Read from the position, much core thinking quietly relies on non-evolution: foundations are here-now positions read as forever-after. If the reality we describe has evolution, the question becomes *which reality?* The field's version of the corrosion is a doorway line page (below); this angle is the position's own and belongs here.
- **Evolutionary thought filed under subjectivism** *(Jules, 2026-10-06, brainstorm)*. A tendency to file evolutionary thinking under subjectivism, with a negative slant, so it can be discarded. The move as it shows in the field (from memory, unverified): Moore's naturalistic fallacy (1903) aimed at Spencer's evolutionary ethics; anti-psychologism around 1900 (Frege; Husserl's *Logical Investigations*, and "Philosophy as a Rigorous Science", 1911, on historicism) taking evolutionary accounts of logic as relativism; the genetic fallacy as a filing rule (origin irrelevant to validity); Reichenbach's discovery/justification split (1938); Kuhn's reception (relativism, irrationalism; Lakatos's "mob psychology"); "social Darwinism" as a pejorative covering evolutionary social thought wholesale. The slide: contingent → subjective → arbitrary. Mirror of the foundations point above: both rest on the assumption that standing requires non-evolution. Fairness note: some targets carried bad arguments (Spencer's ethics, social Darwinism); the label outlived its target.
- **Evolution of evolution** *(brainstorm, 2026-10-06)*. Evolution as a "battering ram" against established thinking (Jules's suspicion: it will become a major one). The best-known use, Dennett's universal acid, exempts one thing: the algorithm itself (variation, selection, retention), kept as a fixed, substrate-neutral foundation — a here-now mechanism elevated to forever-after, the move the acid attacks elsewhere. The position's version does not exempt itself: the means of evolving evolve too (direction embedded in a landscape that itself changes). Field material, from memory, unverified: evolvability; Maynard Smith and Szathmáry, *The Major Transitions in Evolution* (1995) — new modes of inheritance, new levels of individuality. Posture: applied as redescription rather than attack; nothing claimed for the filing move to discard.
- **The agnostic stance and context-bound foundations** *(Jules, 2026-10-06, his formulation)*. The seed ties reality to the medium of language. Languages give a natural evolution of concept schemes, which relate back to the way we experience reality. Whether that is "descriptive" is left in the middle: we don't know (if there is an outside view). This agnostic stance allows reality to be explored, in a natural way, under an evolutionary view, working with context-bound foundations compatible with "turtles all the way down". Field resonances to check: Neurath's boat (1932; rebuilding the ship at sea, no dry dock); Quine's web of belief; Sellars on empirical knowledge as self-correcting, "not because it has a foundation"; coherentism vs foundationalism, Haack's foundherentism.
- **Open.** What the static face is if content drifts under a constant name: the name, or the concept as it stands at a moment. And the Historicity seed line "without historicity, existence is a point: bare differentiation, constancy" — on the working reading it may stand as the static face, or need rewording.

### Doorway line page candidate — what evolution unsettles (neutral, the field's voice)

The evolution doorway shows how far evolution reaches as a mechanism (Dennett's "universal acid" on *The criterion* is quoted for scope; Lorenz's evolutionary reading of Kant's a priori is on *Science and knowledge*), not what it dissolves. A line page would carry the corrosion in the field's own words, case by case, without scoring. **Material, from memory, unverified:**

- **Fixed forms and final causes** — Dewey, "The Influence of Darwin on Philosophy" (1909): philosophy's two-thousand-year reliance on fixed forms, attacked at its home ground, the species; the change in how questions are asked rather than in the answers.
- **Essences** — Mayr's typological vs population thinking (1959); overlaps the species problem on the Being and becoming doorway, decide which page holds it.
- **Eternal truths about man** — Nietzsche, *Human, All Too Human* I §2: lack of historical sense as the family failing of philosophers; everything has become, no eternal facts. Word test: the German is *geworden* ("become"), not "evolved", whatever some translations say.
- **Moral foundations** — Street's "Darwinian dilemma" for moral realism (2006).
- **Reason itself** — Darwin's "horrid doubt" letter (1881) on trusting the convictions of a mind developed from a lower animal's; Plantinga's evolutionary argument against naturalism turns it on naturalism.
- **Fixed meanings** — already carried by *Language change*; link, don't repeat.

Rules: headlines, not details; word test on every author; attribute every judgement to a named source.
