## 2. Research round — evolution in non-Western philosophies and cultures

**Purpose.** Find where traditions outside the Western line have ideas of cosmic or living change that the doorway's sort can place: unfolding (morphology or arrow), recurrence, descent, or something the sort does not anticipate. Then decide where any of it belongs in the bundle, if anywhere.

**Leads to check (all unverified; listed so the round has a starting map, not as findings):**
- **India.** Sāṃkhya's *pariṇāma*, the manifestation of the tattvas from *prakṛti*, conventionally translated "evolution"; the cosmic cycles of the yugas and kalpas as recurrence; Buddhist dependent origination; the nineteenth- and twentieth-century readings of the avatars as an evolutionary sequence (to be treated as reception, not as the texts); Aurobindo's evolution of consciousness.
- **China.** The *Zhuangzi* passage on a chain of transformations from seeds to creatures to humans, often cited as anticipating evolution; the *Yijing* and the vocabulary of change (*bian*, *hua*); Neo-Confucian *qi*; and the word's own arrival — Yan Fu's translation of T. H. Huxley as *Tianyan lun* (1898) and the term *jinhua*.
- **The Islamic world.** Al-Jāḥiẓ's *Book of Animals* and the claims made for it; the Ikhwān al-Ṣafāʾ and Ibn Miskawayh on a graded series from mineral to plant to animal to human; Ibn Khaldūn's passage on the same series in the *Muqaddimah*; Rūmī's "I died as a mineral"; the nineteenth-century Muslim reception of Darwin.
- **Elsewhere.** The Hawaiian *Kumulipo* chant, often compared to an evolutionary sequence; the successive suns of Mesoamerican cosmology as recurrence; Japanese reception of the word (*shinka*); African cosmologies, where the round should look before assuming there is nothing to find.

**Guards.**
- **Word test.** For each case, ask whether the tradition has a term of its own for the change, and separate it from the translator's choice. *Pariṇāma* rendered "evolution", or *tianyan* chosen for Huxley, are facts about translation and reception, and belong with the word's travels, not with the texts' own claims.
- **No priority claims.** Statements that a tradition "had Darwin first" (made for al-Jāḥiẓ, the *Zhuangzi*, the avatars and others) are part of the reception and are reported as such, with their critics. The bundle sorts meanings; it does not award precedence.
- **Neutral register, is-like.** The doorway's voice applies: each tradition in its own terms, placed on the sort only where its own terms support the placement.
- **Backing governs placement.** Decide the destination after the round: a paragraph on existing pages (The word, Progress and direction, Evolution in metaphysics, Civilisation as morphology), a new line page, or the translation-theory bundle for the word's travels. No page by default.

**Output.** A research note with per-claim confirmed/unverified marks, sources, and a placement proposal for review.

## 3. Blog post — the turn to an evolving cosmos

**Thesis.** The modern turn was not the discovery that the world changes — Heraclitus had that — but that its change is historical: it accumulates and goes somewhere. The word's own history carries the turn.

**Three positions as the spine.**
1. *Being:* Parmenides; Aristotle's eternal heavens; Newton's cosmos; Einstein adding the cosmological constant in 1917 to keep the universe static.
2. *Becoming without history:* Heraclitus's fire in measures; Empedocles' cycle; Hutton's "no vestige of a beginning"; Lyell's steady state; Hoyle's steady-state cosmology.
3. *Becoming with history:* the word turning from the unrolling of what is given (*evolutio*, the preformed germ — "all is given", in Bergson's diagnosis) to Spencer's law and Darwin's open history; Hubble and Lemaître; the cosmic background radiation (1965) and the end of the steady state; stars and minerals with life courses; Chaisson's cosmic evolution; Prigogine's *From Being to Becoming*.

**Close.** Short, first person, is-like: what it means that the working image of the sciences moved to Heraclitus's side and then past him. No claim that a position was vindicated.

**Shape.** About 850 words, no headings, image template. Labels: positioning or conversations (to be decided; positioning keeps it apart from the scheduled emergence and entropy word posts). Park on a 2030 date until placed.

**Depends on.** §1 built and committed. Facts to check before drafting: Einstein 1917 and the cosmological constant; the steady-state theory (Bondi, Gold, Hoyle, 1948) and the 1965 discovery; Hubble 1929 and Lemaître 1927; Prigogine's title and date (1980).

**Links.** The Evolution hub, The word, Progress and direction, Cosmic evolution, the Heraclitus page, Process philosophy.

## 4. Further post candidates

Each needs its own backing check before drafting; anything a post says goes onto a reference page first.

**a. Blind or sighted — the first idea and the pursuit.** The bundle's standing dispute, Campbell's blind variation and selective retention against Thagard's reasoned generation of hypotheses (1980), reframed across a lifetime: the guiding idea often arrives blind — a question someone grows up with or stumbles on, with no foresight of where it leads — and is then pursued by sighted means, reasoning, mathematics and experiment, for decades. Thagard describes the pursuit; Campbell the first idea. Worked case: Einstein's question at sixteen about riding alongside a light beam, which he says fed into special relativity (*Autobiographical Notes*, 1949) — used as his own retrospective account, with John Norton's "Chasing the Light" (2013) on what the thought experiment could and could not have shown. Neighbours: Simonton's degrees of blindness; Reichenbach's distinction between the contexts of discovery and justification (not yet on the site; would need backing on the Reichenbach page or the philosophy-of-science subject). Register: the two-stage reading is offered as a way of seeing the dispute, is-like, not as its resolution. Series: positioning.

**b. Schleicher — evolution as an exploration.** A post on the episode itself, framed as an attempt rather than a verdict: a Hegelian linguist, drawing language trees since 1853, reads the *Origin* and offers Darwin, in an open letter to Haeckel (1863), what Darwin lacked — a real tree built from evidence; Darwin cites him in the *Descent of Man*. The attempt is an exploration of what "evolution" could mean when carried between fields: the tree travelled and lasted, the organism with its life cycle was set aside, and the attempt to classify human groups by language is part of the record and is stated as such. Backing: the Schleicher page and Language change. Series: conversations.

**c. Mokyr — diversity of ideas as the engine.** Mokyr's account of why sustained growth began in Europe, approached from ideas and freedom of thought rather than politics: a Republic of Letters in which many competing ideas could be voiced, tested and kept or dropped on their merits, a market for ideas in which no single authority settled what could be thought, and the Industrial Enlightenment's belief that open inquiry into nature would yield useful knowledge, carried by many minds rather than one plan. Diversity and freedom of thought as the condition of innovation, read alongside the pluralism material — is-like, not as Mokyr endorsing a position he does not hold, and with Allen's and Pomeranz's rival explanations kept in view. Backing: the Mokyr page; the pluralism subject. Topical after the 2025 Nobel prize. Series: positioning or conversations, to be decided.

**Not yet decided:** a Toynbee portrait (Thucydides in 1914, the rise and fall of his reputation); "Selection widened" (Chaisson and Hazen's widening of selection against Hazen's own "quartz does not out-compete olivine"); "Lamarckian" as the name fields give to inheritance that is not blind. **Dropped:** Lloyd Morgan portrait; physics' undecided arrow.
