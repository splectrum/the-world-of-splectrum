## 6. Life as a meta subject

*Opened 2026-10-07 as a working document of its own (from the handoff of that session); gathered here 2026-10-11. Same shape as the language and vocabularies documents.*

### 6.1 Aim

To work out how a neutral meta subject (keyword doorway) for **life** should be shaped: the field's material on what life is, including the field's own stretches of "living" beyond the cellular case, gathered and organised so the diversity becomes visible, in the field's voice, with SPLectrum-inspired categorisation. Origin of life is one part of it.

### 6.2 How it came up

- The evolution doorway already carries assembly theory (`/positioning/subjects/a/assembly-theory/`) and mineral evolution (`/positioning/subjects/m/mineral-evolution/`, and the evolution bundle's `mineral-evolution.md`), plus chemical evolution (the bundle's `chemical-evolution.md` — Calvin, Eigen; the doorway frames it as where the unfolding sense hands over to descent). Origin of life is where these meet, but the site has no page of its own for it.
- **[A]** Rather than an origin-of-life page, go for life as a meta subject, with origin of life as part of it.
- **[C]** Reasons it fits:
  - Life is a keyword (the position's "self-sustaining differentiations"); origin of life is a domain, not a keyword.
  - `plan/life/seed-and-origin-of-life.md` already argues that "origin of life" is a misleading framing — there is no clean line, and "life begins here" is a language choice. A page titled *Origin of life* would bake that framing in.
  - Parallel with cognition: "when does chemistry become life" is the same boundary problem as "when does processing become cognition", and cognition already has a doorway.
  - Position-side material exists: `plan/life/seed-and-origin-of-life.md`, `plan/life/sketch-seed-native-definition-of-aliveness.md`.

### 6.3 The author's suspicion

- **[A]** A really good life meta subject will include living systems that we don't categorise as life — mineral evolution points to one. It would be life of a different nature, not our "lego blocks" version of it.

### 6.4 Where the field itself stretches "life" — [C, all unchecked]

- **Functional information.** Hazen, Wong et al., the law of increasing functional information (2023) — minerals, stars and life under one selection law (already on the site). Against it, Hazen's own guard: "quartz does not out-compete olivine" (also on the site).
- **Gaia** (Lovelock, Margulis) — the planet as a self-regulating system; contested precisely on whether "living" applies (the teleology charge).
- **Sara Walker** — life as what information does; the technosphere counted as part of life (some of this already on her person page).
- **Assembly theory** (Cronin, Walker) — a substrate-neutral measure applying to any object; its subject page names origin-of-life research as its strongest empirical domain.
- **Organisation-based definitions** — autopoiesis (Maturana and Varela; a subject page exists), Kauffman's autocatalytic sets, Rosen's closure.
- **Artificial life** — Langton's "life as it could be" against "life as we know it" (1989): the field naming the lego-blocks contrast in its own words.
- **Dissipative structures** (Prigogine) — convection cells, flames: the classic near-misses.
- **Smolin's cosmological natural selection** — universes that reproduce.
- **Meaningful information and agency from physics** — **[C, checked via OpenAlex and the arXiv abstract, 2026-10-11]** Rovelli's "Meaning = Information + Evolution" is cited in the physics-of-life line: Jeffery, Pollack and Rovelli, "On the Statistical Mechanics of Life: Schrödinger Revisited" (2019); Ilan, "The constrained disorder principle defines living organisms…" (2022); synthetic cells extracting semantic information (2023, 2024); and Murray, Marshall and Cronin, "Defining Pathway Assembly and Exploring its Applications" (2018) — an early assembly-theory paper, a direct thread between the essay and the assembly cluster. His "Agency in Physics" (arXiv:2007.05300, 2020) closes: agency "transforms low entropy into information. This may be the general mechanism at the source of the whole information on which biology builds."
- **The definitions-of-life literature** (to research for rule 5): working definitions (e.g. NASA's), Cleland on the impossibility of defining life, Trifonov's survey of definitions — recalled, not checked.

### 6.5 Candidate categorisation (SPLectrum-inspired, in the field's words)

- **[C]** Sort by what each home takes life to be:
  - **substrate** — cells, carbon, DNA (the lego-blocks version);
  - **organisation** — self-production, closure;
  - **process or information** — selection, functional information, assembly;
  - plus a branch for the **boundary cases** the field itself names as candidates for "living": minerals, planets (Gaia), flames, the technosphere, languages, economies.
- Possible further branches (sketch only): definitions of life; origin of life; life as information; life as organisation; life and environment (co-evolution — Hazen's coupled mineral–life history); life elsewhere and artificial life.

### 6.7 What needs care

- **Rule 5 — the word check.** The field does use "life" itself (definitions of life, the nature of life, artificial life, astrobiology) — likely the easy case, like emergence and cognition. Confirm in research, including whether the field's stretches are meant literally or as metaphor.
- **Rule 2 — backing before doorway.** See §9.
- **Guard: the near-miss cases carry their critics with them.** Gaia's reception is the warning — "living" applied to a planet invited the charge of teleology. The doorway reports the field's stretches; it must not read as building a case.
- **Sibling, not facet.** Life is a sibling doorway of evolution, cross-linked (as AI is to cognition). Evolution's origin-of-life handover (chemical evolution) is the bridge.
