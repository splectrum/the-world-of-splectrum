# Scoping: the shared voice — the reality-side page, and the post coupled to it

Working document, opened 2026-09-18. A scoping document: what the page would say, why it is owed, what is already built that it joins up, and what is deliberately deferred. It does not write the page and it does not settle the seed-side questions.

Two deliverables, coupled: a reality-side page, and the post that leans on it. The page comes first — new material flows down into the reference surfaces before a post states it (`process/backing-flows-down.md`).

## 1. What the page is for

**Jules's framing (2026-09-18): this is what makes the position *interrelational* pluralism rather than pluralism of floating voices.**

The site's own word has been carrying a structural claim without a reality-side page saying what it consists of. Floating-voice pluralism has many voices and nothing between them, and cannot explain how anyone reads anyone else, or how coexistence is more than coincidence. The shared voice is what is between — and it is built by the very interaction the position holds to be primary.

`/seed/interrelational-pluralism/` covers the seed side, where the project gets its philosophical name. The reality side has never stated what the interrelation consists of at the content level. That is the gap.

## 2. The claim

**Common understanding is a language — a shared voice.**

The reasoning, in the order it arrived:

- Convergence is not a standard but an *outcome*, checkable from where subjects stand. So objective means converged within what is commonly understood, and no criterion is asked to hold above the games.
- What crosses a language boundary is *understanding*, not *agreement*. Two languages can reach common understanding and disagree entirely — which is the sign that no ranking has been smuggled in.
- Common understanding requires the tools to express it, and tools are what a language is. So implicitly or explicitly it maps to a language; there is no intermediate layer where understanding is shared but unexpressed. P0 reaches the same conclusion directly: a common understanding is held between subjects, so it has a language.
- Therefore the shared voice is **a language game in its own right** — its own moves, its own competence, its own boundaries — not a neutral medium. Each subject stands to it as to any game it does not wholly own.

**Jules's formulation to carry:** we each have our own voice, but our language in common is strong enough for coexistence, and that common understanding is a *shared voice*.

## 3. What it joins up — all already built

The page is a joining-up and a deepening, not a new claim from nothing. Four surfaces already use the material, and none of them links to the others on this point.

- **`reality/epistemology/`** — convergence as the dynamic of learning, the same by which we come to agree on the meaning of a word; explicitly not the pragmatist's convergence of inquiry, sitting a layer deeper in the tools any inquiry runs on; a community can converge on what proves false, which is the ordinary condition of knowing; the discipline is the coupling itself; **"converged subjectivity is the only objectivity there is."** This is convergence *within* a community's tools.
- **`reality/ethics/`** — common understanding as the reference point for reading across games: "the shared base that makes the reading possible at all", with the soundness criterion (anchored in what is genuinely common; unsound where the reader's non-shared content is imported as though shared). This is common understanding *between* games.
- **`reality/politics/`** — the balance among language games establishing itself through common understanding.
- **The 08-08 post** (*Pluralism and the Interrelational*) — answers the relativism objection with two reasons, the second being that the dynamic of coexistence between communities explores convergence and divergence "from their base of common understanding".

**The finding that makes the page worth writing:** common understanding currently does its work in the realm of *coexistence* (ethics, politics). Making it a language puts it in *realisation* as well. The same concept in both realms is either a real unification or a sign the two uses are not quite the same thing — and the page is where that is settled, in the direction of unification.

## 4. Scope — the point in principle, not the detail

Jules: the point of the position in principle and where it is different; not going into detail on all the aspects yet.

So the page says: what the shared voice is; that it is a language and not a medium; that it is built rather than given; what it makes possible (reading across games, coexistence, convergence without a standard above the games). Around 700–900 words, matching the section.

**Register guard.** The page states what the shared voice is and does. It does *not* argue against floating-voice pluralism — the contrast is a clause, not a section. The reality side is not where SPLectrum argues with the field; the post is where the contrast can be drawn out.

**Placement.** A new page rather than an extension of `reality/epistemology/`. It spans realisation and coexistence, which is the point of it, and a pillar page absorbing it would bury that. Title and path undecided — `/reality/common-understanding/` and `/reality/the-shared-voice/` are both candidates, and the territory-naming test applies.

**Coordination that will be needed.** "Common understanding" is a working term on the ethics and politics pages. Whatever the new page says must not leave those drifting — check them when it is written, and amend rather than let two definitions stand.

## 5. The coupled post

Scaffolded on Putnam's three realisms — the angle is not on the site yet.

**Why the scaffolding works.** A real career and a real failure, not a scheme invented for the purpose. Scientific realism, internal realism, natural realism; the third abandoning the second because its standards went relative; and a fourth option he did not take. Putnam's difficulty stated accurately: internal realism replaced correspondence-truth with what would be ideally rationally acceptable within a scheme, but the criteria — coherence, simplicity, empirical adequacy — are themselves stated within a scheme, so the standard goes relative too. He needed a standard holding *across* schemes and could not have one from inside any.

The position's answer is structurally different: convergence is not a standard but an outcome, so the regress does not start.

**Series:** positioning, not conversations — it argues a position rather than drawing a portrait.

**Relation to the 10-08 post** (*The Man Who Kept Changing His Mind*): that one is the portrait and stays clear of the position — wrong register, and it was written before this material existed. The new post is the follow-up that uses the same three positions for a different purpose. Both can stand; the later one should not re-tell the career, only use it.

**Ordering:** page first, post second.

## 6. Deferred, and not needed for this

**The seed side (Jules, 2026-09-18: too early, and the page can be written without going there).** A metalanguage of the common, and a shared language particular subjects built to talk across theirs, are not obviously the same layer. The coin page's perimeter material is adjacent — the seed's domain being the common, its own edge coinciding with the structure's. The shared voice is something *built* by converging; whether that is the seed's common described from the content side, or a different thing, is a further question. Aspects will end up on the seed side; the reality page does not wait for them.

**The coin page's envelope.** That page already holds an envelope for pluralism — the seed side holding it open, equal standing as unbacked ranking. The shared voice is a different kind: content-side, built, particular, achieved rather than structural. They are unlikely to conflict, but both are called an envelope, and the relation should be made deliberate when the seed-side work opens.

**Decoherence.** The framing arrived through the decoherence rhyme — what crosses is what survives coupling with independent others. That stays a candidate mechanism held as research under belonging, with its two guards (relational/RQM reading only; is-like at full force). **The shared-voice claim does not depend on the physics and must not be made to.**

**Aesthetics.** Expression is where shared tools are worked into what one has to say, so the mapping-to-language step is aesthetics' territory as much as epistemology's. Not developed here.
