## 1. Purpose

- **[A]** The reality section is SPLectrum's philosophy. The real life section is a place where we *talk* about what is in the strict sections — seed, positioning, reality, and, to come, language and vocabularies.
- **[A]** The site is organised in bite-size pages, without any attempt at a longer text or a longer story — in fact the opposite. Real Life is where we could go against these rules: a section for more accessible pages.
- **[A]** Candidates: SPLectrum 101; where to talk about SPLectrum from the foundations; commentary on topics like philosophy, science and belief. The start-here page belongs in this topic too.
- **[C]** The strict sections are deliberately a quarry — non-sequential, not bent toward book structure. That leaves the longer telling without a home; Real Life gives it one, so the strict sections never have to carry sequence or story. Longer Real Life pieces are close to the "trail documents" once thought of as the cheap test of a book structure: told paths through the strict pages.

- **[A]** The `spl/` folder is about Real Life applied to the project: pages that clarify the project.
- **[A]** Other applications get their own place as they arise — an article about beliefs in philosophy could go under a "philosophy" part; another example: "Are humans privileged?". No further structure is set yet, only how it is seen evolving.

## 2. Rules proposed

- **[C] Says nothing the strict sections don't hold.** Real Life talks about what the strict sections contain; it holds no position of its own. A new idea flows down into seed, reality or positioning first, and the Real Life piece then tells it. This keeps the section from becoming a back door for theory-building, and keeps the non-contradiction rule between posts and reference pages workable.
- **[C] Standing, maintained pages — not the blog.** The blog is the dated conversation stream, trying ideas out; Real Life pieces stand and are kept current. Review trigger: when a strict section a piece talks about changes shape, the piece is checked.
- **[C] Outside the hierarchy, as a commentary layer.** Real Life links into every strict section; the strict sections do not link out to it (the home page and landing pages excepted).
- **[C] Longer and accessible.** Sequence, narrative and plain language allowed — the opposite of the strict sections' bite-size discipline.
