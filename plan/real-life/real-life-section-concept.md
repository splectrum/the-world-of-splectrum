# The Real Life section — concept

*Working document, opened 2026-10-12. The section's purpose as it came out of discussion; to be agreed before the section's tone-of-voice guidance is rewritten.*

## How to read this document

- **[A]** — the author's claim or decision, verbatim or near-verbatim.
- **[C]** — a formulation supplied by Claude in discussion, to be confirmed.

---

## 1. Purpose

- **[A]** The reality section is SPLectrum's philosophy. The real life section is a place where we *talk* about what is in the strict sections — seed, positioning, reality, and, to come, language and vocabularies.
- **[A]** The site is organised in bite-size pages, without any attempt at a longer text or a longer story — in fact the opposite. Real Life is where we could go against these rules: a section for more accessible pages.
- **[A]** Candidates: SPLectrum 101; where to talk about SPLectrum from the foundations; commentary on topics like philosophy, science and belief. The start-here page belongs in this topic too.
- **[C]** The strict sections are deliberately a quarry — non-sequential, not bent toward book structure. That leaves the longer telling without a home; Real Life gives it one, so the strict sections never have to carry sequence or story. Longer Real Life pieces are close to the "trail documents" once thought of as the cheap test of a book structure: told paths through the strict pages.

## 2. Rules proposed

- **[C] Says nothing the strict sections don't hold.** Real Life talks about what the strict sections contain; it holds no position of its own. A new idea flows down into seed, reality or positioning first, and the Real Life piece then tells it. This keeps the section from becoming a back door for theory-building, and keeps the non-contradiction rule between posts and reference pages workable.
- **[C] Standing, maintained pages — not the blog.** The blog is the dated conversation stream, trying ideas out; Real Life pieces stand and are kept current. Review trigger: when a strict section a piece talks about changes shape, the piece is checked.
- **[C] Outside the hierarchy, as a commentary layer.** Real Life links into every strict section; the strict sections do not link out to it (the home page and landing pages excepted).
- **[C] Longer and accessible.** Sequence, narrative and plain language allowed — the opposite of the strict sections' bite-size discipline.

## 3. Candidate pieces

- **SPLectrum 101** — live; rebuilt 2026-09-07 as the coin (part one the common, part two the own voice). Open threads in the 101 discussion capture: disclosure as engagement (the language a subject offers), how far the game imagery carries, the senses without an input pipeline.
- **SPLectrum from the foundations** — the working draft in this folder: part one the common (three rooms; five how-constraints — being is tension, historicity, mixing, sedimentation, coupling), part two own-voice readings (reality, aliveness, truth/objectivity/correctness, meaning, belonging), the status of the common. Before it can be a Real Life page it must agree with the strict sections:
  - the five how-constraints against the seed principles P0–P5 — two formulations of the same face;
  - its truth/objectivity reading ("truth is what converges") against the 2026-10-12 finding that convergence is belief and knowledge a matter of seat;
  - its meaning reading ("meaning lives in the circulation… the game holds the meaning") against the finding that meaning ends up in concepts;
  - its pending fence links (the Meaning reading, the coupling constraint).
- **Philosophy, science and belief** — commentary. Material: convergence as the view of the others; philosophy full of beliefs; more languages give more robust convergence, not knowledge.
- **The start-here page** (`/start-here/`, "Reading Path") — the site's orientation for new readers; currently a top-level page linked from the home page.
- **The 9-box homepage** — already in the section.
- **The 404 page** (`docs/404.html`) — a plain placeholder since the move to Cloudflare Pages; to become a page that helps a lost reader find their way into the site.
